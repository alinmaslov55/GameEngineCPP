## 1. Executive Summary & Before-vs-After Health

The 30-package remediation campaign dramatically improved engine stability, memory safety, and test coverage. Most of the original 22 Critical blockers—including `C-01` (`SaveManager` `.bak` recovery), `C-03` (`JobSystem` Init/Shutdown race), `C-04`/`C-05` (Box2D 64-worker lease bitmask & `Box2DTask` lifetime), `C-07` (`RemoveComponent<Component>` `m_Transform` guard), `C-09` (`Animator` `m_PlayGeneration`), `C-11` (`EditorLayer` UUID selection & drag-drop), `C-13` (`SDL_Unbind/BindAudioStream`), `C-16` (`#version 410` line shader), `C-17` (`GL_TEXTURE6` guard), `C-18`/`C-19`/`C-20` (`DDSLoader`, light sort transitivity, `SpriteAtlas` async UVs), and `C-22` (UTF-8 `Font`, `UIButton` focus guard)—are now structurally resolved.

## 3. Critical Findings (Must-Fix Blockers)

### `CRIT-01` — Heap Use-After-Free & Write-After-Free in `GameObject::RemoveComponent<T>()` *(Regression from `FIX-ECS-01`)*
- **Location:** [GameObject.h](file:///c:/DEV/GameEngineCPP/Engine/include/Engine/Scene/GameObject.h#L475-L553), [GameObject.cpp](file:///c:/DEV/GameEngineCPP/Engine/src/Scene/GameObject.cpp#L50-L122), [PhysicsWorld2D.cpp](file:///c:/DEV/GameEngineCPP/Engine/src/Physics/PhysicsWorld2D.cpp#L417-L449)
- **Root Cause:** On every normal (`m_ComponentIterationDepth == 0`) removal of an active, enabled component, lines 485–488 wrap `comp->OnDisable()` in a local `ScopedComponentIterationGuard`. When that inner `guard` exits at line 488, depth drops `1 → 0`, immediately calling `FlushPendingComponentRemovals()` which runs `comp->OnDestroy()` and **`delete`s `comp`**. Lines 490, 491, 524–527, and 530 then dereference the freed `comp` pointer (`GetTypeID()`, `GetBaseTypeID()`, `NotifyPhysicsComponentRemoved(..., comp)` writing `RuntimeBody.Reset()`, and `HasOverriddenFixedUpdate()`).
- **Fix:** Hoist `ScopedComponentIterationGuard guard(*this);` to span lines 479–552, snapshot `removedExactID`, `removedBaseID`, and `hasOverriddenFixedUpdate` before `OnDisable()`, and remove the inner guard and manual `FlushPendingComponentRemovals()` call at lines 549–551.

### `CRIT-02` — Worker-Thread `~Texture()` (`glDeleteTextures`) & Wrongful Cache Eviction *(Incomplete `C-02` / `FIX-CORE-01`)*
- **Location:** [ResourceManager.cpp](file:///c:/DEV/GameEngineCPP/Engine/src/Core/ResourceManager.cpp#L448-L465) (plus lines 490–505, 533–549)
- **Root Cause:** In `handleFailure()`, `auto target = placeholderWeak.lock();` creates a strong `shared_ptr<Texture>` on the worker thread. (1) If `cache[cacheKey]` was replaced (`it->second != target`), `target` drops at line 460 and can run `~Texture()` (`glDeleteTextures`) on the worker thread with no GL context. (2) When `!target` is true (old placeholder already expired), `if (!target || it->second == target)` **wrongfully evicts a newly reloaded valid texture** from the cache! (3) Success paths also hold strong `target` on the worker thread.
- **Fix:** Never `.lock()` `placeholderWeak` on worker threads. Compare ownership via `!placeholderWeak.owner_before(it->second) && !it->second.owner_before(placeholderWeak)`, move `it->second` directly into `QueueTextureGarbage`, and store `std::weak_ptr<Texture>` inside `PendingTextureUpload` to be locked on the main thread in `ProcessPendingUploads()`.

### `CRIT-03` — Unlocked Raw `AudioStreamTrack*` Use-After-Free in `AudioManager::Update()` *(Incomplete `C-14` / `FIX-AUD-01`)*
- **Location:** [AudioManager.cpp](file:///c:/DEV/GameEngineCPP/Engine/src/Audio/AudioManager.cpp#L359-L366), [AudioStreamTrack.cpp](file:///c:/DEV/GameEngineCPP/Engine/src/Audio/AudioStreamTrack.cpp#L20-L23), [MusicDirector.cpp](file:///c:/DEV/GameEngineCPP/Engine/src/Audio/MusicDirector.cpp#L436-L447)
- **Root Cause:** `UpdateInternal()` copies raw `AudioStreamTrack*` pointers into `tracksCopy`, releases `s_AudioMutex`, and then invokes `track->IsPlaying()`, `track->IsAutoUpdateEnabled()`, and `track->Update()` unlocked. Concurrent destruction of a track (e.g., `MusicDirector::Shutdown()`) immediately unregisters and frees `track` while `UpdateInternal()` dereferences it.
- **Fix:** Synchronize stream-track registry iteration and destruction with a dedicated `s_TrackRegistryMutex` (ordered after `s_MusicMutex`, before `m_TrackMutex`) or ref-counted weak tokens so `~AudioStreamTrack()` cannot free a track mid-`Update()`.

### `CRIT-04` — Dangling Raw `GameObject*` in `Scene::m_PointLightEntities` & Terrain Cache During `FlushEntityLifecycleQueues` / `Scene::~Scene()` *(Introduced in `OPT-01`)*
- **Location:** [Scene.cpp](file:///c:/DEV/GameEngineCPP/Engine/src/Scene/Scene.cpp#L111-L148), [Scene.cpp](file:///c:/DEV/GameEngineCPP/Engine/src/Scene/Scene.cpp#L291-L386), [Scene.cpp](file:///c:/DEV/GameEngineCPP/Engine/src/Scene/Scene.cpp#L860-L865)
- **Root Cause:** (1) `RegisterPointLightEntity` does not reject `PendingDestroy`/`Destroyed` entities, and `FlushEntityLifecycleQueues` has no post-`OnDestroy()` sweep of `m_PointLightEntities`, so adding a `PointLightComponent` inside `OnDestroy()` leaves a freed `GameObject*` in `m_PointLightEntities`. (2) `Scene::~Scene()` clears `m_PointLightEntities` and nulls `m_CachedChunkManager`/`m_CachedTerrain` **after** the `while` loop frees entity batches; any multi-pass `OnDestroy()` query hits freed pointers.
- **Fix:** Guard `RegisterPointLightEntity` against `PendingDestroy`/`Destroyed`, add a post-`OnDestroy()` `std::erase_if(m_PointLightEntities, ...)` in `FlushEntityLifecycleQueues()`, and clear `m_PointLightEntities` + terrain cache pointers **before** (and after each pass of) `Scene::~Scene()`'s teardown loop.
