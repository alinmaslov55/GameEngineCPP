[ ] 3. Fix Box2D Contact Begin-Event Approach Speed, RemoveComponent Body Leaks & Capsule Mismatch
    - Files: Engine/src/Physics/PhysicsWorld2D.cpp (lines 269-319, 825-866, 905-910, 1039-1056), Engine/src/Physics/PhysicsComponents.cpp (lines 103-141, 254-283, 330-338, 382-388, 456-484, 626-629, 666-692), Engine/include/Engine/Scene/GameObject.h (lines 495-545)

Because `b2Manifold::normal` points from shape A to shape B, approaching bodies have `(vA - vB) . normal > 0`, so the negation clamps approaching impacts to `0.0f` and scores separating bodies positive.
      * `GameObject::RemoveComponent<T>()` calls `OnDisable()` and clears `m_ComponentMask` before calling `NotifyPhysicsComponentRemoved` and `comp->OnDestroy()`: removing `Rigidbody2D` on a collider-less entity causes `UnregisterRuntimeBody` (`:270`) to see `GetComponent<Rigidbody2D>() == nullptr` and leak the `b2BodyId`, while `Collider2D::OnDisable()` calls `DisableRuntimeBody()` which disables the entire shared `b2BodyId` even when `Rigidbody2D` or sibling colliders remain enabled. `DisableRuntimeBody()` also ignores standalone `Collider2D` and `Tilemap` bodies.
      * Degenerate `CapsuleCollider2D` (`halfLength <= 1e-5f`) creates a Box2D circle shape internally while runtime updates unconditionally call `b2Shape_GetCapsule`/`SetCapsule` (asserting in Box2D). `BoxCollider2D` updates compare only `vertices[0]`.

      * `Rigidbody2D::OnPoolRelease()` clears `m_IsStasisFrozen` without restoring `b2Body_SetType(bodyId, b2_dynamicBody)`, `SetStasisFrozen(true)` flips `Static` bodies to kinematic, and `RefreshMassFromShapes()` while frozen measures `0` mass and overwrites `m_BaseMass = 1.0f`.
    - Box2D v3 API Constraint:
      * Box2D v3 has no `b2Shape_Disable()`. To disable a single `Collider2D` without freezing a shared `Rigidbody2D` or sibling `Collider2D`, destroy `RuntimeShape` (`b2DestroyShape`) or zero its `b2Filter::maskBits` on `Collider2D::OnDisable()` and recreate/restore on `OnEnable()`; only call `b2Body_Disable()` when no enabled `Rigidbody2D` and no other enabled `Collider2D` remain on the `GameObject`.
    - Solution:
      * Remove the minus sign in the `beginEvents` fallback: `std::max(0.0f, relVel.x * normal.x + relVel.y * normal.y)`.
      * Notify physics before clearing `m_ComponentMask` (or pass the component/body handle directly), detach individual `RuntimeShape`s in `Collider2D::OnDisable`/`OnDestroy` when a body is shared, and handle standalone `Collider2D` + `Tilemap` in `DisableRuntimeBody()`.
      * Guard degenerate capsule lengths in update, compare both box extents (`vertices[0]` and `vertices[2]`), invalidate `m_StaticColliderCacheDirty` on any static collider add/remove, and restore dynamic body type in `OnPoolRelease()` while preserving `m_BaseMass` during stasis.
    - Verification (EngineTests): Assert positive `ApproachSpeed` on sub-threshold `beginEvents` impacts, zero Box2D body leaks on `RemoveComponent<Rigidbody2D>()`, multi-collider disable/remove without freezing `Rigidbody2D`, degenerate capsule runtime resize, and stasis-frozen pool reuse.
